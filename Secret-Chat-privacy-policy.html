1. Introduction & Zero-Knowledge Philosophy

SecretChat ("we", "our", or "the app") is engineered from the ground up on the core principle of Zero-Knowledge Architecture. We believe private, unmonitored communication is a fundamental human right.

SecretChat operates 100% serverless. We do not own, operate, or lease any centralized messaging or database servers. Consequently, it is architecturally and mathematically impossible for us to record, store, log, inspect, monetize, or sell your conversations, media, contacts, IP addresses, or metadata.

Core Guarantee: What happens on your device stays on your device. Messages are exchanged directly phone-to-phone via WebRTC DataChannels with zero intermediate servers.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

SecretChat does not collect, log, track, share, or upload any of the following:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We never ask for your real name, phone number, email address, username, or personal profile.
  • No Contacts Book Access: We never read, inspect, or upload your device's address book or contact lists.
  • No Location Data: We do not track, collect, or share your GPS coordinates, Wi-Fi location, or precise physical location.
  • No Device or Ad Identifiers: We do not collect IMEI numbers, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), or hardware fingerprints.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: There are zero advertising SDKs, analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), crash telemetry, or behavioral monitoring tools embedded in the app.
  • No Central Message Logs: Messages and view-once media never pass through any central proxy or cloud database.

3. How P2P Communications Work (WebRTC & E2EE)

SecretChat establishes direct peer-to-peer communication between devices:

  • Direct P2P Transport: Devices communicate directly via WebRTC DataChannels over local or direct peer networks.
  • Military-Grade Cryptography: All communications are end-to-end encrypted (E2EE) using X25519 (ECDH key agreement) and authenticated AES-256-GCM with unique 96-bit Initialization Vectors (IV) per packet.
  • Digital Signatures: Authenticity and integrity are validated through Ed25519 digital signatures.
  • Offline QR Handshake: Users can exchange cryptographic connection keys face-to-face via compressed QR payloads without touching any external signaling server.

4. On-Device Sandbox & Stealth Camouflage

Because all data lives on your device, SecretChat includes multi-layered physical and forensic security protections:

  • Calculator Camouflage: The application launches disguised as an authentic, fully functional dark-slate Scientific Calculator. The secret vault opens only upon entering your private PIN followed by the equals sign (e.g. 143=).
  • Anti-Coercion Cover: Entering a secondary decoy PIN (e.g. 0000=) displays harmless, everyday decoy notes to protect against forced disclosure.
  • Instant RAM Zeroing: When viewing "view-once" photos or burner media, the memory buffer in device RAM is actively overwritten with zeros (buffer.fill(0)) the instant your finger releases the screen, defeating physical RAM dump forensics.
  • Cryptographic Shredding: Disappearing messages are permanently deleted and purged from the local SQLite database upon expiration of the burner countdown timer.
  • Android FLAG_SECURE: Active system protection blocks screenshots, screen recording, and task-switcher previews of your confidential chats.
  • Zero Gallery Indexing: Received media is kept in the app's encrypted local sandbox and is never indexed by Android MediaStore or public gallery applications.

5. Optional Google Drive Cloud Vault

Users may optionally back up and restore their encrypted vault using personal Google Drive storage:

  • Restricted Scope: SecretChat uses Google OAuth 2.0 PKCE requesting only the narrow https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope. The app has zero access to your personal Drive files, documents, or photos.
  • Client-Side Zero-Knowledge Encryption: Before any backup payload leaves your phone, it is encrypted locally using AES-256-GCM with a 256-bit key derived from your Master PIN via PBKDF2 (100,000 iterations).
  • Google Cannot Read Your Data: Even Google LLC cannot decrypt or inspect your backup files, because the encryption keys never leave your device.

7. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Since we do not store any user data on servers, you maintain complete and absolute ownership over your data at all times:

  • Instant 1-Tap Wipe: You can permanently shred all contacts, messages, cryptographic keys, and database records at any time directly in the app via Settings → Purge Vault.
  • Disappearing Timers: Ephemeral messages automatically expire and shred themselves based on your selected timer (5s, 30s, 1m, 5m, 1h).
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling SecretChat immediately deletes the local encrypted SQLite sandbox from your Android device.
