Our Core Principles

Designed with Ethics & Integrity

We believe mobile utilities should empower users, not exploit their attention or harvest their private data.

🔒

Strict Local Privacy

Every file you convert, barcode you scan, or activity you track stays confined to your phone's sandbox storage. No hidden server uploads or third-party data selling.

⚡

Lightweight Architecture

Zero background battery drain. Minimal APK sizes, instant cold starts, and hardware-accelerated rendering using modern Android Jetpack libraries.

No Dark Patterns
