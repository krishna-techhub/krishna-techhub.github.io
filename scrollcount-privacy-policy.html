01

Commitment & Application Scope

Welcome to the official Privacy Policy for ScrollCount : Reels & Shorts Tracker (Application ID: com.techhub.scrollcount), engineered and published by Krishna Tech Hub.

ScrollCount is an offline-first digital wellbeing utility created to help conscious smartphone users understand, measure, and curb doom-scrolling habits across short-video platforms (Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts). We believe your digital wellness data is strictly personal. Our core architecture guarantees that your screen time numbers belong exclusively to you.

Our Foundational Guarantee

ScrollCount operates without central user databases, without cloud synchronization, and without tracking SDKs. Everything is calculated, rendered, and maintained directly on your phone's processor.

02

Prominent Disclosure: Android AccessibilityService Usage

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy regarding Accessibility APIs, we provide this comprehensive, upfront disclosure explaining our exact use of android.permission.BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE:

Why ScrollCount Requires AccessibilityService

ScrollCount uses the Android AccessibilityService API solely to detect when Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts player viewports are actively visible and playing on your screen. This is the only technical mechanism Android provides to accurately calculate short-form video consumption without rooting your phone.

Technical Functionality & Operation

  • ✔
    Targeted UI Node Inspection: The service listens for window state changes strictly to recognize the UI layout containers of Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
  • ✔
    Smart 3s+ Qualification Algorithm: Rapid accidental swipes (under 3 seconds) are filtered out so that only videos you actually pause and watch are counted toward your daily scroll quota.
  • ✔
    Duplicate Protection: Re-watching a looping video is recognized so that repeat views of the same clip are not falsely double-counted.

Strict Privacy Boundaries — What We NEVER Access

To ensure complete trust and security, the Accessibility Service operates with strict programmatic limitations:

  • 🚫
    NO Chat or Message Monitoring: The service never reads, accesses, logs, or stores direct messages (DMs), chats, comments, or personal conversations.
  • 🚫
    NO Keystrokes or Password Logging: The service never monitors text input fields, passwords, PINs, search queries, or banking details.
  • 🚫
    NO Audio, Video, or Screen Recording: The service does not capture audio streams, video frames, or screenshots of the content being viewed.
  • 🚫
    NO Interception Outside Short-Video Containers: When you navigate away from Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts (e.g. to your home feed, profile, or any other app), tracking is instantly dormant.

User Consent & Control: The Accessibility Service can be toggled on or off at any moment by navigating to Settings → Accessibility → ScrollCount on your Android phone.

03

Absolute Offline Guarantee: Zero Internet Permission

Unlike most wellbeing apps that upload analytics to cloud dashboards, ScrollCount takes an uncompromising architectural stance:

Verified AndroidManifest Declaration

ScrollCount's AndroidManifest.xml does NOT declare android.permission.INTERNET. Under Android's security sandbox model, an application without this permission is physically prevented by the Linux operating system kernel from opening internet sockets, making HTTP requests, or transmitting data.

Even if an attacker or malicious code existed in the application, it is technically impossible for ScrollCount to communicate with remote servers or leak your statistics.

05

Android Permissions Breakdown

ScrollCount requests the minimum set of permissions necessary to function:

BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE

Video Session Detection

Detects when Reels or Shorts video containers appear in the foreground to measure elapsed watch time and count scrolls. Never captures chats, audio, or passwords.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+)

Habit & Limit Alerts

Used solely to display local reminder notifications when you reach your self-imposed daily scrolling threshold (e.g. "30 minutes reached today").

INTERNET (NOT REQUESTED)

Network Access: Disabled

This permission is omitted from the application manifest. The app has zero network capability and cannot transmit data outside your physical phone.

06

Local Data Retention & Erasure

All generated statistics (number of videos watched per day, time spent per platform, custom limit thresholds) are saved in your device's private SQLite database:

  • ✔
    Private Application Sandbox: Data is locked inside Android's internal app sandbox (/data/data/com.techhub.scrollcount/), inaccessible to other apps installed on your phone.
  • ✔
    Instant 1-Tap Reset: You can reset your stats or delete all recorded days at any point from ScrollCount's in-app settings.
  • ✔
    Complete Erasure on Uninstall: Uninstalling ScrollCount automatically erases all databases, preferences, and cached statistics completely from flash storage.
